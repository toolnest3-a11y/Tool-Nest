Professional Image Rotation & Flipping Tool

Our Advanced Image Rotator & Flipper is a comprehensive online solution for adjusting image orientation with precision control. Whether you need to correct photos taken at the wrong angle, create mirror effects for design projects, or prepare images for social media and websites, our tool provides seamless rotation and flipping capabilities with real-time preview. The intuitive interface allows you to rotate images by any angle from 0° to 360° with pixel-perfect accuracy.

The tool supports all major image formats including JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, and BMP. With precise angle control (0-360°), preset rotations (45°, 90°, 180°, 270°), horizontal and vertical flipping, and quality output settings, you can efficiently adjust multiple images for any purpose. All processing happens locally in your browser, ensuring complete privacy and security for your images - no uploads to external servers required.
