Professional Image to Text Conversion Tool

Our Advanced Image to Text Converter is a powerful online OCR (Optical Character Recognition) solution that transforms images into editable text with exceptional accuracy. Whether you need to extract text from photographs, scanned documents, PDF files, screenshots, or handwritten notes, our tool utilizes state-of-the-art machine learning algorithms to deliver precise text extraction results.

The converter supports over 100 languages including English, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Japanese, Arabic, Russian, and many more. With advanced settings for formatting preservation, table detection, image enhancement, and handwriting recognition, you can customize the extraction process to match your specific requirements. All processing happens locally in your browser, ensuring complete privacy and security for your sensitive documents.
