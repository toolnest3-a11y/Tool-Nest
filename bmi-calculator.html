Understanding BMI Categories

< 18.5
Underweight
May indicate nutritional deficiencies, osteoporosis risk, or underlying health conditions. Consider consulting a healthcare professional for dietary advice.
18.5 - 24.9
Normal Weight
Associated with the lowest risk of health problems. Maintain through balanced diet and regular exercise for optimal health and longevity.
25 - 29.9
Overweight
Increased risk of developing health conditions. Consider lifestyle changes including diet modification and increased physical activity.
≥ 30
Obesity
Significant health risks including heart disease, diabetes, and hypertension. Professional medical advice recommended for weight management.
