PDF Security API

Integrate PDF encryption directly into your applications with our REST API.

Endpoint: POST /api/v1/encrypt-pdf

Headers: Content-Type: multipart/form-data

Parameters:

file: [PDF file]
password: [encryption password]
encryptionLevel: "128" or "256" (default: "256")

Response:

  "success": true,
  "encryptedFile": "base64_encoded_pdf",
  "fileName": "protected_document.pdf",
  "encryptionLevel": "256-bit AES",
  "fileSize": "2.5 MB"
