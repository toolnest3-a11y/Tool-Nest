Professional Image Rotation & Flipping Tool

Our Advanced Image Rotator & Flipper is a comprehensive online solution for adjusting image orientation with precision control. Whether you need to correct photos taken at the wrong angle, create mirror effects for design projects, or prepare images for social media and websites, our tool provides seamless rotation and flipping capabilities with real-time preview.

The tool supports all major image formats including JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, and BMP. With precise angle control (0-360°), preset rotations (90°, 180°, 270°), horizontal and vertical flipping, and batch processing capabilities, you can efficiently adjust multiple images for any purpose. All processing happens locally in your browser, ensuring complete privacy and security for your images.

Perfect for photographers, graphic designers, social media managers, e-commerce store owners, and content creators who need to optimize image orientation for better presentation. Correct landscape/portrait issues, create symmetrical designs, prepare images for printing, or simply fix incorrectly oriented photos from your camera or smartphone.

Image Transformation API

Integrate our powerful image rotation and flipping functionality into your applications with our REST API. Perfect for developers building photo editing apps, CMS platforms, or digital asset management solutions.

POST https://api.imagetransformer.com/v1/transform
Content-Type: multipart/form-data
Body: {
  image: [file],
  rotation: [0-360],
  flip_horizontal: [boolean],
  flip_vertical: [boolean],
  quality: [10-100],
  format: [jpeg|png|webp|gif],
  batch: [boolean]
}

Response: {
  success: true,
  transformed_url: "https://cdn.imagetransformer.com/processed/xyz.jpg",
  original_orientation: "landscape",
  transformed_orientation: "portrait",
  rotation_applied: "90",
  flip_applied: {horizontal: true, vertical: false}
}
