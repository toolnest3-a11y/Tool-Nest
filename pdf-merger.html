    PDF Processing API

    Integrate PDF merging and splitting directly into your applications with our REST API.

    Merge Endpoint: POST /api/v1/merge-pdfs

    Headers: Content-Type: multipart/form-data

    Parameters:

    files: [Array of PDF files]
mergeOrder: "asc" or "desc" (default: "asc")

    Split Endpoint: POST /api/v1/split-pdf

    file: [PDF file]
pages: "1,3-5,7" (page numbers to extract)
