Image Resizing API

Integrate our advanced image resizing technology directly into your applications with our REST API. Perfect for developers building CMS platforms, e-commerce systems, or digital asset management solutions.

POST https://api.toolnest.com/v1/resize

Headers:
Content-Type: multipart/form-data
Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY

Parameters:
- image: File to resize (required)
- width: Desired width in pixels (required)
- height: Desired height in pixels (required)
- maintain_aspect_ratio: true|false (default: true)
- quality: 10-100 (default: 85)
- format: jpg|png|webp|gif|bmp (default: png)
- scale_percentage: 10-200 (optional)
- batch: true|false (default: false)

What is Image Resizing?

Image resizing is the process of changing the dimensions of a digital image while maintaining or optimizing its visual quality. Unlike cropping (which removes portions of an image), resizing scales the entire image proportionally to new dimensions. Our tool uses advanced interpolation algorithms to ensure that resized images maintain sharpness, clarity, and color accuracy across different scale factors.
