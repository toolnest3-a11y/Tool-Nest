Base64 Encoding Standards

Standard Base64

Most common encoding using A-Z, a-z, 0-9, +, / and = padding. Used in MIME email, XML, JSON, and HTTP headers.

Character Set: 64 Padding: =

Base64URL

URL-safe variant using - and _ instead of + and /. No padding with = characters. Perfect for URLs and filenames.

URL Safe: Yes Padding: None

MIME Base64

Email attachment encoding with line breaks every 76 characters. Used for email attachments and MIME types.

Line Length: 76 Format: MIME
