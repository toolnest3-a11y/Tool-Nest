About Indian GST System

India has a multi-tier GST structure with rates of 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, and 28%. For intra-state transactions, CGST and SGST are applicable, while IGST applies to inter-state transactions.

The GST system in India replaced multiple indirect taxes, creating a unified tax structure across the country.

Understanding GST Calculation Methods:

Exclusive GST Calculation: GST is added to the base amount. Formula: GST = Base Amount × (GST Rate/100). Total = Base Amount + GST.

Inclusive GST Calculation: GST is already included in the total amount. Formula: GST = Total Amount × (GST Rate/(100 + GST Rate)). Base Amount = Total Amount - GST.

Common GST Rates by Country:

  • India: 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28% (with CGST/SGST split or IGST for inter-state)
  • Australia: 10% (GST applies to most goods and services)
  • Canada: 5% (GST) + provincial taxes (PST/HST varying by province)
  • Singapore: 9% (current GST rate effective 2024)
  • Malaysia: 6% (SST - Sales and Service Tax)
  • United Kingdom: 20% (VAT standard rate with reduced rates for essentials)
  • New Zealand: 15% (GST on most goods and services)
  • Japan: 10% (consumption tax with reduced 8% rate for certain items)
