Supported Barcode Formats

CODE128

Universal barcode format supporting all 128 ASCII characters. Ideal for logistics, shipping, and general purpose use.

Characters: 128 Type: Alphanumeric

EAN-13

International standard for retail products. Used globally for product identification and point-of-sale systems.

Digits: 13 Type: Numeric

UPC

North American standard for retail products. Widely used in United States and Canada for consumer goods.

Digits: 12 Type: Numeric

CODE39

Industrial barcode format supporting letters and numbers. Common in automotive and government applications.

Characters: 43 Type: Alphanumeric

ITF-14

Shipping container barcode for carton-level identification. Used in logistics and supply chain management.

Digits: 14 Type: Numeric

Pharmacode

Pharmaceutical tracking barcode for medicine packaging. Designed for high-speed scanning in healthcare.

Digits: 1-6 Type: Numeric
